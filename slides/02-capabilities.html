Video

Motion is
design information.

A still image cannot show sequence, delay, easing, or how the page feels while it scrolls. Use video to inspect that evidence and to make the finished page easier to share.

01
Review motionRecord or use a short clip of the reference hero. Inspect the order and timing frame by frame.
02
Match the buildRecord the rebuilt hero and compare its motion to the reference before you call it done.
03
Reuse the resultTurn the finished page into a short product clip with voice-over, captions, and a simple composition.
